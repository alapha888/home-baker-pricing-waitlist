# Home Baker Pricing Spreadsheet by Channel — Demand Validation Page

A planned pricing spreadsheet for home bakers and cottage-food sellers who sell the same bakes through several channels (Etsy, Instagram/direct, farmers market): recipe cost plus packaging, spoilage, and each channel's fee stack, so the estimated margin per channel sits side by side for the same product. Intended price $14.99 one-time.

- Validation page: https://alapha888.github.io/home-baker-pricing-waitlist/
- Status: product not built. This repo contains only the validation page — no product files.
- Decision line: 20 signups within 30 days (by 2026-11-13) → start full production; below that → drop the idea and notify every signup by email.
- Intended price (validation parameter): $14.99 one-time. No payment, no pre-order.
- Demand evidence: overseas-category assessment 2026-10-14 (goal hidden_files/海外版评估-备婚烘焙-20261014.md) — baking cost spreadsheets are an established paid category on Etsy (at least 5 comparable listings found first-hand at $6.99–$9.99), but those are recipe-cost focused and no differentiation was found for a translated generic version. The untested angle here is per-channel margin: the same product's estimated margin after each channel's fee stack, stall-fee allocation and spoilage. This page tests exactly that hypothesis.
- Copy review: antigravity (Gemini 3.8 Flash Medium) reviewed the hypothesis 2026-10-14: per-channel margin judged a credible differentiator (most sellers anchor all channels to one sticker price); two fixes adopted — "true margin" replaced with "estimated margin after known fees and per-order variable costs, before labor, rent and taxes", and the $14.99 price is now stated on the page explicitly against the $6.99–$9.99 recipe-only band so signups judge the premium directly.
- Relation to the Chinese version: the Chinese home-baking cost/pricing sheet waitlist (alapha888/baking-cost-waitlist, ¥19.9/¥39.9) stays live under its own decision line; the two pages test different markets and different hypotheses.
