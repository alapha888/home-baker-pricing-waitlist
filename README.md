# Home Baker Pricing Spreadsheet by Channel — Demand Validation Page

A planned pricing spreadsheet for home bakers and cottage-food sellers who sell the same bakes through several channels (Etsy, Instagram/direct, farmers market): recipe cost plus packaging, spoilage, and each channel's fees, so pre-built estimated margin scenarios per channel sit side by side for the same product. Intended price $14.99 one-time.

- Validation page: https://alapha888.github.io/home-baker-pricing-waitlist/
- Status: product not built. This repo contains only the validation page — no product files.
- Decision line: 20 signups within 30 days (by 2026-11-13) → start full production; below that → drop the idea and notify every signup by email.
- Intended price (validation parameter): $14.99 one-time. No payment, no pre-order.
- Demand evidence: overseas-category assessment 2026-10-14 (goal hidden_files/海外版评估-备婚烘焙-20261014.md) — baking cost spreadsheets are an established paid category on Etsy (at least 5 comparable listings found first-hand at $6.99–$9.99), but their core is recipe costing, some already include packaging or marketplace-fee inputs, and no differentiation was found for a translated generic version. The untested angle here is narrower: pre-built per-channel scenario comparisons — the same product's estimated margin after each channel's fees, stall-fee allocation and spoilage under assumptions the seller sets. This page tests exactly that hypothesis.
- Copy review: antigravity (Gemini 3.8 Flash Medium) reviewed a $19.99 version of the hypothesis 2026-10-14 and judged the pain point credible but the moat weak at a 2–3x premium. Its full delayed reply flagged three overclaims, all corrected on the page: existing calculators are not all recipe-only (some include packaging or marketplace-fee inputs); stall-fee and spoilage outputs are scenario estimates, not a "true" margin; and "fee stack" was jargon for ordinary fee inputs. The page now frames the offer as pre-built multi-channel scenario comparisons and time saved, not exclusive math, and tests a lower $14.99 price against the $6.99–$9.99 band.
- Relation to the Chinese version: the Chinese home-baking cost/pricing sheet waitlist (alapha888/baking-cost-waitlist, ¥19.9/¥39.9) stays live under its own decision line; the two pages test different markets and different hypotheses.
